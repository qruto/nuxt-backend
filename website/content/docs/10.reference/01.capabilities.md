This page lists every capability with what it owns: its env names, the `doctor` findings that check it, its docs home, its DevTools tab and its playground pages. Use it to find the capability a variable or a finding belongs to. [Capabilities](/docs/start/concepts/capabilities) explains the idea, and [`npx nuxt-backend status`](/docs/reference/cli#status) shows each capability's state. A capability's state is detected from the env and `doctor`'s checks, never configured.

## What changes

`0.2.0` has twelve capabilities: `auth`, `data`, `jobs`, `billing`, `email`, `notifications`, `ai`, `protection`, `monitoring`, `flags`, `analytics` and `hosting`. The `simulated` state is now `test`, and each capability's development mode is its provider's test mode: Polar's sandbox, Resend's test addresses read back in the dev inbox, and the Convex AI Gateway:

| Change | Unit |
|---|---|
| `notifications` arrived with the in-app inbox. Web push, with `PUSH_PUBLIC_KEY` and `PUSH_PRIVATE_KEY`, joins it | PREFS |
| `analytics` arrived with revenue metrics in the admin console, the growth funnel and experiments | OPS2, FUNNEL |

The mode variables are one, `BACKEND_ENVIRONMENT`, owned by `data`. `auth` lost `AUTH_TRUST_LOCAL_ORIGINS` and `NUXT_BACKEND_LOG_OTP`, `data` lost `NUXT_PUBLIC_BACKEND_SITE_URL`, `billing` lost `BILLING_ENVIRONMENT` and `BILLING_SIMULATOR_URL`, `email` lost `EMAIL_TEST_MODE` and gained `EMAIL_MARKETING_FROM` and `EMAIL_REGION`, `ai` lost `NUXT_BACKEND_SIMULATE_MODELS` and `monitoring` lost `MONITORING_ENVIRONMENT`.

The new features join capabilities that exist:

- **`auth`**: launch phases, invite codes and access links, the waitlist, and consents with a minimum age.
- **`data`**: checked uploads, storage refs and share links (FILES), and retention (RETAIN).
- **`ai`** and **`jobs`**: the generation queue (JOBS).
- **`protection`** and **`ai`**: moderation of uploads and prompts, and user reports (SAFETY).
- **`auth`**: codes bound to the browser that asked, passkey-only accounts (AUTHSEC), and the minimum age.
- **`billing`**: the cancel flow, renewal reminders and the withdrawal button (LIFECYCLE).
- **`ai`**: content credentials and the AI label (PROVENANCE).
- **`monitoring`**: the error-reporting preset (personal data scrubbed, log lines, replay after consent, the feedback widget), the email quota and AI spend alerts, and AI cost per person in the admin console.
- **`hosting`**: the packaged legal pages, filled from the capabilities the app uses.

The packaged UI is not a capability: its pages and components follow the capabilities they serve, and its stylesheets load only for those detected.

[Environment variables](/docs/reference/configuration/environment-variables) lists the `0.2.0` names by group, and [Diagnostics](/docs/reference/cli/diagnostics) the findings each unit adds or removes.

## States

| State | Light | Means |
|---|---|---|
| `live` | green | The real service is configured, and the capability acts for real. |
| `test` | amber | The capability runs in its service's test mode: a dev deployment, the email provider's test mode or the billing sandbox. |
| `missing` | grey | What it needs is not configured, so it degrades the way its env names describe. |
| `failing` | red | A check of the capability failed, whatever else holds. |
