The components a person meets around their account, from signing in or joining the waitlist to deleting the account. Most render a ready-made page too, so each section names the page as well as the component. Each one keeps the [component contract](/docs/reference/components#the-component-contract): its props, slots, events, states and accessibility are listed in its section.

## What changes

`0.2.0` adds two components, removes one and extends two:

- **`<ConsentPrompt>` arrives.** It asks for the consents a person has not given at their current version, the age of an account that never answered it, and, while the app uses optional storage, one question per browser. Each answer is a row of the consent ledger. Its section is below.
- **`<WaitlistForm>` arrives.** It joins the waitlist, confirms the address with a sign-in code and shows the person's place, a link to share and a way to leave. Its section is below.
- **`<AuthForm>` gains steps.** It renders every step `useLoginFlow` adds, each with a slot named after the step: `undeliverable` for an address the email provider stopped delivering to, `age` for a new address of an app with a minimum age, and `waitlisted` and `invite-code` for a closed launch phase, where `waitlisted` shows the place on the waitlist. It also shows the agreement line of the required consents, the `age` step when the app sets a minimum age, and after a code sign-in the `passkey-offer` step, which offers to save a passkey. Its regions are [parts](/docs/reference/components/parts#sign-in-authform), such as `AuthFormCode`, that a same-named component of yours replaces.
- **`<AccountData>` deletes with a grace period.** Deleting schedules the deletion instead of running it, emails a "Keep my account" link, and shows the date with a button that cancels it. Its `deleted` event is now `scheduled`, and the new `kept` event fires when the account is kept. The export adds the account's stored files, as zip parts behind links that end with the export's.
- **`<SecuritySettings>` turns passkey-only on and off,** behind a recent sign-in, and shows the recovery code a passkey-only account recovers with. Its new Alerts section (`SecurityAlerts`, the `alerts` slot) lists the latest sign-ins from a new device, each signing its session out in one click while it lasts.

`<ProfileSettings>` and `<ReauthenticateDialog>` keep their props, slots and events, and `<SecuritySettings>` keeps its own and adds the `alerts` slot.
