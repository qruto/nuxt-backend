The components that manage workspaces, read the activity trail and run the [admin console](/docs/build/auth/admin). Each one keeps the [component contract](/docs/reference/components#the-component-contract): its props, slots, events, states and accessibility are listed in its section.

## What changes

`0.2.0` keeps these components and changes what they stand on:

- **Built-in roles only.** `<WorkspaceSettings>` and `<AcceptInvitation>` read `useWorkspace()`, and no label, event or error they show says "organization". A workspace keeps the built-in roles; custom roles, ownership transfer and invite links wait for the next release ([Roadmap](/docs/start/roadmap)).
- **The impersonation banner becomes a notice (SIMPLIFY).** `<ImpersonationBanner>` stops being a public component and lives on as the `impersonation` notice of [`<BackendNotices>`](/docs/reference/components/shell#backendnotices).
- **The admin console gains tabs.** Beside Users, Workspaces, Activity, Flags, Access (launch phases, invite codes and access links, shown once the app has `backend/access.ts`), Waitlist (the waiting people, admitted in waves, shown once the app has `backend/waitlist.ts`), Inbox (the contact form's messages and inbound mail, with replies to the support inbox, shown once `backend/email.ts` re-exports `getInboxConsole`) and System, the package adds a tab for each feature that brings an admin section: Queue for generation jobs (JOBS), Analytics with its Revenue, Growth and Experiments views (shown once the app re-exports `getRevenue`, `getFunnel` or `getExperiments`), Reports for the content review queue (SAFETY) and Webhooks for replaying deliveries (OPS1).
- **Apps add tabs of their own:** a page of yours under the console's path with the `adminTab` page meta joins the console, guarded like the console itself, and a page guarded with `hidden: true` answers 404 to anyone it refuses ([Add your own admin tabs](/docs/build/auth/admin/custom-tabs)).

`<AuditLog>`, `<AdminUsers>` and `<AdminWorkspaces>` keep their props, slots and events.

## `<AdminUsers>`

Every account of the app, for its administrators: a search by address, a page at a time (`AdminUsersTable`), and a drawer with one account in full (`AdminUserDrawer`): its role, its ban, its device sessions and workspaces, and "Impersonate". Setting a role, banning, lifting a ban and impersonating take a recent sign-in, which the component's own `<ReauthenticateDialog>` asks for; signing an account's sessions out does not. An administrator's own account offers none of these, and another administrator's offers no impersonation.
