Every word the packaged pages, components and emails show is a label. This page lists each label group and key with its English default, so you can override or translate the exact sentence you see. An app overrides one as `labels.<group>.<key>` in [app config](/docs/reference/configuration/app-config) or in a component's `labels` prop, and translates it as the `backend.<group>.<key>` message of `@nuxtjs/i18n`. [Change copy and labels](/docs/build/ui/customize/labels) says which source wins, and [Translate the UI](/docs/build/ui/customize/translation) how messages are found.

**Params** are the `{name}` values a message fills in. **Forms** is the number of plural forms, separated by `|`, which `count` picks between. The message names are stable. Their params and the English wording may change in a `0.x` release, so override a key to pin a sentence ([Stability](/docs/ship/releases/stability)).

## What changes

The groups follow the components, so they change where the components do:

- **The banner groups stay.** `<BackendNotices>` words its notices from the groups the banners used: `impersonation`, `maintenance`, `credits` and `gift` (SIMPLIFY).
- **Each new component brings a group of its own**: the notifications inbox, the contact form, the upload field and the app shell have theirs. A notification's own words are in the `notifications` group too, as a `title` and a `body` under the kind (`workspace.member_removed.title`), and an app's own kind adds its words under its type without `app.` ([Notify people about your own events](/docs/build/email/notifications/custom-events#give-it-words)).
- **No label says organization.** Every word names a workspace.
- **The `simulated` layer state reads `test`** in the admin console's system tab (NOSIM).
