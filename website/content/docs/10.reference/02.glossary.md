The package names things for what they do, and the services underneath have words of their own. Each term below has an anchor, its upstream word where it has one, and the page that explains it. A term a unit brings names that unit: the code has it once the unit lands ([Feature map](/docs/start/feature-map)).

## Access link

A link that admits a cohort of people to the app while sign-ups are not public, such as everyone at a meetup. An administrator creates it with a label, a maximum number of uses and an expiry, and can revoke it. The token is stored hashed and travels in the URL fragment. Arrives with ACCESS. See [Hand out invite codes and access links](/docs/build/growth/launch/invites).

## Active workspace

The workspace a signed-in person is acting in, carried in their session as a claim, so every query and mutation knows it without an argument. `useWorkspace()` switches it. Upstream: Better Auth's active organization. See [Workspaces](/docs/build/auth/workspaces#the-active-workspace-is-a-claim).

## Activity trail

The one record of what happened to an account and inside a workspace: sign-ins, workspace changes, an administrator's actions and billing events, with your own events beside them. Each row holds ids, never an address. Upstream: none; it is the package's. See [Record and review activity](/docs/build/auth/admin/audit-log).

## App env and deployment env

The two places configuration lives: the Nuxt app's own env (the backend's URL and the challenge's site key) and the deployment's (`AUTH_SECRET`, `SITE_URL`, `BACKEND_ENVIRONMENT` and every provider key). `npx nuxt-backend env push` forwards the deployment's part from `.env.local`. See [Environments and deployments](/docs/start/concepts/environments#the-apps-env-and-the-deployments).

## Backend component

The package's one Convex component, `backend`, mounted in `backend/convex.config.ts`. It owns its tables (accounts, workspaces, the billing cache, email records, the activity trail) and their maintenance, so your app registers no cron for them. Upstream: a Convex component. See [How it works](/docs/start/concepts/how-it-works).

## `BACKEND_ENVIRONMENT`

The variable that says what kind of deployment the backend runs on: `development`, `preview`, `sandbox` or `production`. Email test mode, Polar's sandbox, trusted local origins, sign-in codes in the logs and the monitoring environment all follow from it. `env push` derives it from `CONVEX_DEPLOYMENT`, and you set `sandbox` by hand for a demo or staging app on the providers' test modes. See [Environments and deployments](/docs/start/concepts/environments#backend_environment).

## Backend event

Something that happened in the backend, such as a sign-in, a workspace change or an administrator's action, written once to the activity trail, where everything that reacts to it reads it. See [Events](/docs/reference/backend/events#backend-events).

## Billing entity

Who pays: the active workspace by default, or each person with `billTo: 'user'`. Its plans, credits and features are shared by everyone it covers. Upstream: a Polar customer. See [Decide who manages billing](/docs/build/billing/setup#who-may-buy-for-a-workspace).

## Capability

A part of the app named for what it gives, such as auth, billing or email: `0.2.0` has twelve. It was called an app layer in 0.1, and "layer" now means only Nuxt's `extends`. A capability's state is detected as `live`, `test`, `missing` or `failing`, never configured (`test` replaces `simulated` with NOSIM), and `doctor --only` selects its checks by name. See [Capabilities](/docs/start/concepts/capabilities) and the [capability reference](/docs/reference/capabilities).

## Consent

A person's recorded yes to one versioned kind, such as the terms of service or the launch newsletter, in a ledger that keeps grants and withdrawals. Its subject is the account, or a hash of the address before sign-up, and a consent given before sign-up carries over to the account. It joins the account's export and erasure. See [Record consents](/docs/build/auth/accounts/consents).

## Credit

One unit of a prepaid balance on a meter, granted by a plan every cycle or by a pack once, and spent by your functions. Upstream: meter credits. See [Credits](/docs/build/billing/credits).

## Customization ladder

The seven ways to change the packaged UI, from least to most work: mount it, restyle it with tokens, change its labels, fill its slots, swap a part, build on the headless composables, and eject. Each rung keeps everything below it working. See [Customize the UI](/docs/build/ui/customize).

## Dedicated slot

One of the queue's running places kept for a queue tier and the tiers listed above it, taken out of the queue's concurrency, also called a reserved slot. Free jobs never take one, and at least one slot always stays shared. It is not a credit reservation. See [Give plans priority in the queue](/docs/build/ai/jobs/priority#keep-slots-for-paid-plans).

## Dev inbox

The messages your dev deployment sent to Resend's test addresses, read back through Resend's emails API: `npx nuxt-backend inbox` in the terminal, `useSandboxInbox` in a page. You, a coding agent or an end-to-end test use it to finish an email-code sign-in with no mailbox. It replaces the sandbox inbox that stored its own copies (NOSIM). See [Read what was sent in the dev inbox](/docs/develop/sandboxes#read-what-was-sent-in-the-dev-inbox).

## Eject

`npx nuxt-backend eject <Part>`, `eject page:<key>` or `eject email:<name>` copies the source of a part, a packaged page or an email into the app, stamped with the package version it came from, so `upgrade` can show what changed upstream. The last rung of the [customization ladder](#customization-ladder). See [Eject a part, page or email](/docs/build/ui/customize/eject).

## Feature

A key a plan includes, such as `priority_support`, that the UI gates and your functions enforce. Upstream: a feature-flag benefit. See [Feature gating](/docs/build/billing/subscriptions/feature-gating).

## Feature flag

A switch declared in code, read inside your queries and mutations, and targeted by account, workspace, plan or a stable percentage. A **kill switch** is a flag that pauses sign-ups, checkout or AI. See [Roll out features and pause services](/docs/ship/operate/flags).

## Finding

One result of `doctor`'s checks, with an id, a capability, a status (`pass`, `warn` or `fail`), a message and its fix. Each id has an entry at `/d/<id>`. See [Diagnostics](/docs/reference/cli/diagnostics).

## Gift

Credits, or a plan for a number of months (a plan pass), bought for someone else by email: the purchaser pays, and the recipient claims it after signing in. The gifts page lists what a person sent and received. See [Gifts](/docs/build/growth/offers/gifts).

## Grace period

The time between a person asking to delete their account and the deletion itself. During it, a "keep my account" link in the confirming email cancels the deletion. See [Export and delete an account](/docs/build/auth/accounts/data).

## Grant

A feature or credits given to a billing entity because of what it bought, kept in the entitlement cache so it reads live. Upstream: a benefit grant. See [Where grants come from](/docs/build/billing/subscriptions/feature-gating#where-grants-come-from).

## Headless composable

A composable that returns the state, the actions and the bindings of a packaged component, so your own markup behaves the same: `useLoginFlow` behind `<AuthForm>`, `usePricing` behind `<PricingTable>`. A binding carries the accessibility attributes and handlers to spread with `v-bind`. See [Build on the composables](/docs/build/ui/customize/headless).

## Impersonation

An administrator acting inside someone's account or workspace for a short, logged visit, with a notice that says so. It is the only way an administrator acts as someone else. See [Impersonation](/docs/build/auth/admin/impersonation).

## Invite code

A code that admits one person to the app while sign-ups are not public: personal or shareable, stored hashed, with a maximum number of uses, an expiry and a ledger of who redeemed it. The `/invite` page redeems one. Arrives with ACCESS. See [Hand out invite codes and access links](/docs/build/growth/launch/invites).

## Job

One queued run of long work, such as a generation, with a queue tier from the owner's plan, a position in the queue and its progress. Each tier sets how many jobs one owner runs at once, one by default. It holds the credits when the job is queued, charges them when it starts and refunds them when it fails. A page follows one by its id, live. See [Run generation jobs](/docs/build/ai/jobs).

## Launch phase

Who may sign up right now: `early-access`, `invite-only` or `public`. An administrator moves the app from one to the next, and a ready-made `canSignIn` enforces it. Existing accounts always sign in. Arrives with ACCESS. See [Decide who can sign up](/docs/build/growth/launch).

## Meter

What credits are counted on, such as `credits`, declared in the catalog. A plan or pack grants credits on a meter, and spending draws them down. Upstream: a Polar meter. See [Define and sync your catalog](/docs/build/billing/catalog#declare).

## Pack

A one-time purchase of credits on a meter, such as `credits500`. Upstream: a one-time product with a meter-credit benefit. See [Credits](/docs/build/billing/credits).

## Part

One region of a packaged component or page, registered under its own name, such as `AuthFormCode` or `PricingPlanCard`. A component of the same name in your app replaces it everywhere, packaged pages included. See [Parts](/docs/reference/components/parts).

## Personal workspace

The workspace every account gets on its first sign-in, so a person who never creates or joins one still has somewhere to work and to bill. See [Workspaces](/docs/build/auth/workspaces).

## Plan

A subscription, priced by the month, the year or both, with the features and credits it includes. Upstream: one recurring product per price. See [Define and sync your catalog](/docs/build/billing/catalog#declare).

## Promotion

A discount declared in the catalog with its real terms, such as a launch offer, pushed by `billing sync` and applied at checkout by key. Upstream: a Polar discount. See [Run a promotion](/docs/build/growth/offers).

## Quota

How many of a resource a plan allows, such as three projects, counted in the mutation that creates one. Experimental. See [Limit resources per plan](/docs/build/billing/subscriptions/quotas).

## Recent sign-in

A sign-in from the last ten minutes, which a few sensitive actions ask for, in a dialog that resumes the action. Upstream: Better Auth's fresh session age. See [Require a recent sign-in](/docs/build/auth/access/step-up).

## Reservation

The first step of a metered spend: credits are held before the work runs, then settled at the real cost, or released when the run fails, so a failed run costs nothing. A queued job holds them for as long as it waits (JOBS). Upstream: settling sends a usage event. See [Spend credits in your functions](/docs/build/billing/credits/spending).

## Sandbox

A provider's mode for testing, where nothing is charged or delivered for real: Polar's sandbox, Resend's test mode, a challenge's test keys. Every deployment but `production` runs on them, so development needs no simulator. See [Develop on real sandboxes](/docs/develop/sandboxes).

## Scaffold contract

The files the scaffold writes into `backend/`, and the functions the package calls in them by name, which `doctor` checks a deployment for. See [Project files](/docs/reference/configuration/project-files).

## Share link

A link that lets anyone read one row of yours without signing in, such as a finished piece of work. The token is stored hashed, and its owner can enable, rotate or revoke it. Arrives with FILES. See [Files](/docs/build/data/files).

## Test address

An address at `resend.dev` that Resend accepts in test mode and answers with real delivery events: `delivered@`, `bounced@` and `complained@`, each with a `+label` form such as `delivered+agent@resend.dev`. You sign in with one on a test deployment, in the playground and in tests. See [Email on Resend's test addresses](/docs/develop/sandboxes#email-on-resends-test-addresses).

## Tier

Two things are called tiers. A **function tier** is one of the builders `createFunctions` returns, each a drop-in `{ query, mutation, action }` that checks the caller first: `authed`, `workspace`, `admin`, `withRole(role)` and `withFeature(key)` (SIMPLIFY). A **queue tier** is what a job gets in line, taken from the owner's plan feature: a head start, how many jobs the owner runs at once, dedicated slots and a daily credit budget (JOBS). See [Protect your functions](/docs/build/auth/access/functions).

## Workspace

A group of people who share data, a plan and credits, with a built-in role each: owner, admin or member. Better Auth's `organization` and `member` tables hold them inside the component, and no public name says organization. See [Workspaces](/docs/build/auth/workspaces).
