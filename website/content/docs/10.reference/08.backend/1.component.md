The `backend` component is what `app.use(backend)` mounts in `backend/convex.config.ts`. Your app
reaches it as `components.backend.<module>.<function>` (from `./_generated/api`), typed by the
package's `ComponentApi`. Every module below has a helper in the app-side entrypoints that wraps it
with the right arguments and checks — use the helper. Call a component function directly only
where the helper's docs say so, such as sending from a mutation or a workflow step.

## `adapter`

The Better Auth database: the auth tables (users, sessions, accounts, verifications, passkeys,
workspaces and their members and invitations, agent OAuth clients and tokens) behind Better Auth's
adapter interface.

- Functions: `create`, `findOne`, `findMany`, `updateOne`, `updateMany`, `deleteOne`, `deleteMany`.
- Wrapped by `setupAuth` from [`nuxt-backend/auth`](/docs/reference/api/auth): Better Auth calls
  the adapter, and the functions `setupAuth` returns (`getAuthUser`, `listWorkspaces`,
  `listSessions`, …) read through it.

## `email`

Transactional email through the email provider's component, nested inside `backend`, plus the
sandbox inbox test mode keeps.

- Functions: `send`, `status`, `get`, `cancel`, `cleanup`, `cleanupAbandoned`, `handleWebhook`,
  `inbox`.
- Wrapped by `setupEmail` from [`nuxt-backend/email`](/docs/reference/api/email). Call
  `components.backend.email.send` directly from a mutation or a workflow step, which cannot run an
  action: see [Sending email](/docs/email/sending).

## `billing`

The entitlement and credit cache the billing webhooks keep: each billing entity's active products,
benefits and meters, and the reservations a metered call holds until it settles.

- Functions: `getByUser`, `userByCustomer`, `upsert`, `deleteByUser`, `getBenefitMetadata`,
  `upsertBenefitMetadata`, `credit`, `debit`, `settle`, `release`, `finalize`,
  `attachReleaseJob`, `clearPendingSpends`, `clear`.
- Wrapped by `setupBilling` from [`nuxt-backend/billing`](/docs/reference/api/billing):
  [credits](/docs/billing/credits) and [feature gating](/docs/billing/feature-gating) read and
  spend through it.

## `gifts`

Gift purchases: who bought what for which address, and whether it has been paid, notified and
claimed.

- Functions: `create`, `get`, `listByEmail`, `resolveRecipient`, `markPaid`, `markNotified`,
  `markClaimed`.
- Wrapped by `setupBilling`'s gift functions: see [Gifts](/docs/billing/credits/gifts).

## `ai`

The records of metered AI calls: one per request, from reservation to settlement or release.

- Functions: `createRequest`, `getByStream`, `markSettled`, `markReleased`, `clear`.
- Wrapped by `ai.meteredAction` from [`nuxt-backend/ai`](/docs/reference/api/ai): see
  [Metered actions](/docs/ai/metered-actions).

## `webhooks`

The delivery log of the billing and email webhooks, which also makes a replayed delivery a no-op.

- Functions: `record`, `find`, `listRecent`.
- Written by the routes `registerBackendRoutes` mounts from
  [`nuxt-backend/http`](/docs/reference/api/http): see [Webhooks](/docs/ship/production/webhooks).
