The `backend` component is what `app.use(backend)` mounts in `backend/convex.config.ts`. This page lists its modules, its functions and its tables, so you know what the package keeps in your deployment and which helper reaches each part. Your app reaches it as `components.backend.<module>.<function>` (from `./_generated/api`), typed by the package's `ComponentApi`. Every module has a setup helper that wraps it with the right arguments and checks, so use the helper. Call a component function directly only where the helper's docs say so, such as sending from a mutation or a workflow step.

## What changes

The component keeps everything the package stores, and its own maintenance, so your app registers no table and no cron for it. `0.2.0` changes what it holds:

- **Gone:** the sandbox inbox's stored copies. The dev inbox reads sent messages back through Resend's emails API, and the component keeps none.
- **Staying internal:** Better Auth's `organization`, `member` and `invitation` tables keep their names inside the component. Every public name says workspace.
- **Launch access** (`setupAccess`): the phase an administrator set, invite codes and access links (keyed hashes only), invitations by address and the redemption ledger.
- **The waitlist** (`setupWaitlist`): one entry per verified address, the joins waiting for their code, a count per status, and the ids of the provider's segment and topic for launch news. The order people wait in is an aggregate and the launch-news mirror a workpool, both nested in this component (`waitlistRank`, `waitlistMirror`).
- **The consent ledger** (`setupAccount({ consents })`): one row for each time an account, or before it signed up a hash of its address, agreed to something, withdrew it or chose optional storage. Rows are never overwritten.
- **Notifications** (`setupNotifications`): one row for each person an event notified in the app, restating the event by ids and the kind's few values, never the words, and a mark for when it was read. A read one is deleted 90 days after it was read and an unread one 365 days after it was recorded.
- **Email preferences** (`setupEmail({ topics, caps })`): one contact per address the app mails under a topic, with its choice for each topic, its time zone, when it last engaged and its recent marketing sends, and the provider's ids of the topics and the accounts' segment. The choices are erased and exported with the account.
- **Suppressions** (`setupEmail`): a copy of the email provider's suppression list, one row per address, kept from its `suppression.*` and `email.suppressed` events; sign-in reads it before it sends a code, and a lifted address goes a week after the lift.
- **Known devices** (`setupAuth`): one row per device an account signed in from, as keyed hashes of its fingerprint, for new-device alerts; a device goes a year after it was last seen.
- **Arriving**, each with its setup helper: push subscriptions (PREFS); storage refs and share links (FILES); user reports and the review queue (SAFETY).

The modules, functions and tables below are the component as it stands.

## `adapter`

The Better Auth database: the auth tables (users, sessions, accounts, verifications, passkeys, workspaces and their members and invitations) behind Better Auth's adapter interface.

- Wrapped by `setupAuth` from [`nuxt-backend/auth`](/docs/reference/api/auth): Better Auth calls the adapter, and the functions `setupAuth` returns (`getAuthUser`, `listWorkspaces`, `listSessions`, …) read through it.

## `email`

Transactional email through Resend's component, nested inside `backend`, plus the dev inbox: an action that reads a test address's mail back from Resend's log of sent email, and stores nothing.

- Wrapped by `setupEmail` from [`nuxt-backend/email`](/docs/reference/api/email). Call `components.backend.email.send` directly from a mutation or a workflow step, which cannot run an action: see [Sending email](/docs/build/email/sending).

## `billing`

The entitlement and credit cache the billing webhooks keep: each billing entity's active products, benefits and meters, the reservations a metered call holds until it settles, and the count of discount codes the provider refused, per billing entity, in a fixed one-hour window (the codes themselves are not stored). A checkout counts its code before the provider sees it and gives the count back when the code is accepted or never tried. `entityProfile` reads who stands behind a workspace entity, for the provider customer it bills through: its name, its owner and the workspaces that owner owns. `recordSubscriptionChange` keeps what the synced subscriptions table lacks, a subscription's scheduled plan change and resume date, newest first by the provider's `modifiedAt`, and `subscriptionChanges` reads them back for one entity.

- Wrapped by `setupBilling` from [`nuxt-backend/billing`](/docs/reference/api/billing): [credits](/docs/build/billing/credits) and [feature gating](/docs/build/billing/subscriptions/feature-gating) read and spend through it.

## `gifts`

Gift purchases: who bought which pack for which address, on which billing entity, and whether it has been paid, notified, claimed or refunded, with the claim discount.

- Wrapped by `setupBilling`'s gift functions: see [Gifts](/docs/build/growth/offers/gifts).

## `ai`

The records of metered AI calls: one per request, from reservation to settlement or release.

- Wrapped by `ai.meteredAction` from [`nuxt-backend/ai`](/docs/reference/api/ai): see [Metered actions](/docs/build/ai/metered-actions).

## `webhooks`

The delivery log of the billing and email webhooks, which also makes a replayed delivery a no-op.

- Written by the routes `registerBackendRoutes` mounts from [`nuxt-backend/http`](/docs/reference/api/http): see [Webhooks](/docs/ship/security/webhooks).

## `audit`

The activity trail: one row of ids per event (who acted, who it is about, the workspace, the thing acted on, the kind's own fields), read a page at a time for one reader (an account's own, a workspace's, or everything), and erased per account when the account is deleted.

- Wrapped by `setupAudit` from [`nuxt-backend/audit`](/docs/reference/api/audit): the auth layer's write points and `recordBackendEvent` record through it, and its three reads list it. See [Record and review activity](/docs/build/auth/admin/audit-log).
