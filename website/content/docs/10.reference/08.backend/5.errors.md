A refusal from a package function is a `ConvexError` whose data is `{ code, ...fields }`. Convex
sends that data to the client in production too, where any other error arrives as "Server Error".
An auth route refuses with the same code and fields in its error body, at the status below.
[`getBackendError(error)`](/docs/data/errors) reads both and returns `{ code, data, message }`.
`BACKEND_ERRORS` in `nuxt-backend/functions` holds the catalog, and
`test/unit/error-catalog.test.ts` keeps this page in step with it. `/e/<code>` links to the code's
entry below.

`message` is the sentence to show: the throw site's own sentence for the person when it wrote one
(`data.message`), else the code's label from the `errors` group. Some reasons have a label of their
own (`errors.<code>_<reason>`), and a label with numbers in it is used only when the numbers are
there. The English the deployment log shows is the error's `message` property, which production
never sends to the client.

Every field in `data` is optional on the read side, the required ones too: the reader keeps only
the fields that fit the catalog, so check the one you use before you rely on it.

## Codes

### `unauthenticated`

| Data | Label | Status | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| `message?` | Sign in to continue. | 401 | Show the message. If nobody is signed in, send the person to sign in, then retry. |

Better Auth answers some of its own refusals with 401 too, and the reader maps them here with Better
Auth's message: a wrong password on a sign-in form, for one, where the person is already on the
sign-in page and only needs the message.

### `forbidden`

| Data | Label | Status | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| `reason`, `message?` | You don't have access to this. | 403 | Show the message; `reason` says what would change the answer. |

### `no_workspace`

| Data | Label | Status | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| `message?` | Create or choose a workspace first. | 409 | Offer the workspace switcher, or create one. |

### `rate_limited`

| Data | Label | Status | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| `retryAfter?` (ms), `limit?`, `message?` | Too many requests. Try again in a moment. | 429 | Wait `retryAfter`, then retry. `limit` names the spent limit. |

### `insufficient_credits`

| Data | Label | Status | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| `meter?`, `balance`, `need`, `message?` | Not enough credits. Top up to continue. | 402 | Offer a credit pack or a plan with more credits. |

With both amounts, the label reads "Not enough credits: {balance} left, {need} needed. Top up to
continue." (`errors.insufficient_credits_amounts`).

### `invalid_input`

| Data | Label | Status | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| `field`, `message?` | That input isn't valid. Check it and try again. | 400 | Mark `field` and show the message. |

### `not_ready`

| Data | Label | Status | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| `reason`, `message?` | Nothing to show yet. | 409 | Don't offer a retry: something has to happen first. `reason` says what (below). |

### `unavailable`

| Data | Label | Status | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|
| `service`, `reason`, `message?` | This isn't available right now. Try again shortly. | 503 | Retry later only for `provider_refused`; the other reasons need the deployment fixed, and `reason` tells an operator what. |

## Reasons

The `forbidden` reasons the package throws:

| Reason | When |
|---|---|
| `banned` | The account is banned. |
| `role` · `permission` | The account's role lacks the role or the permission statement the call requires. |
| `not_member` · `workspace_role` | The caller is not a member of the workspace, or lacks the workspace role. |
| `admin_only` | A billing admin function (`refundOrder`, the webhook log) and the caller is no admin. |
| `not_owner` | The order, subscription or agent request belongs to another account, or the order does not exist: the two answer alike. |
| `impersonating` | An administrator acting as this account tried something only its owner may do. |
| `sign_in_refused` | `canSignIn` said no; its message is the one shown. |
| `customer_taken` | The email's billing profile already pays for another workspace or account. |
| `billing_profile_mismatch` | The gift sits on a different billing customer than yours; `data.message` asks the sender to re-send it to your billing email. |
| `gift_refused` · `not_recipient` · `no_email` | `canGift` said no, the gift is addressed to another email, or the session has no email. |

A `forbidden` the reader maps from Better Auth's own 403 carries Better Auth's error code, lower-cased,
as its `reason` (`you_are_not_allowed_to_ban_users`, say). Those reasons pass through as Better Auth
names them: they are not in the table above and not covered by the stability promise.

The `not_ready` reasons:

| Reason | When | Label |
|---|---|---|
| `no_customer` | The billing entity has no provider customer yet: the portal, a usage report or a refund needs one, and a first checkout creates it. | Nothing to show yet — this opens after your first purchase. |
| `not_synced` | The credit balance never synced for this entity: no credits have reached it, or a mutation spent with `allowRefresh: false`. `syncEntitlements` or a credit pack changes it. | Nothing to show yet — no credits have reached this account. Open billing to refresh, or get a credit pack. |

The `unavailable` reasons, by service:

| Service | Reason | When | Retry? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `billing` | `not_configured` | `BILLING_ACCESS_TOKEN` is not set on the deployment. Label: "This isn't set up on this app yet." | No |
| `billing` | `provider_refused` | The billing provider refused the request; the log has `billing.provider_failed`. | Yes |
| `email` | `provider_refused` | The email provider refused a call (a segment, a contact, a broadcast); its words are in the error's `message`, for the log. | Yes |
| `email` | `test_mode` · `unconfigured` | A code or an invitation would not arrive: the deployment is in test mode and the address is no test address, or it has no email key. The log has `email.undeliverable`. The message says the address can't receive mail here, or that the app can't send email yet. | No |
| `backend` | `unexpected` | Never thrown: a composable's `error` reads a failure that carries no code this way (a function that crashed, which production words as "Server Error", or an app's own `ConvexError`). It says nothing about whether a service is down. Its `message` is the app's own sentence when it wrote one, else the label "Something went wrong." See [Composable conventions](/docs/reference/nuxt/conventions). | Maybe |

A `rate_limited` from a package limit names it in `limit`: `emailOtp`, `emailOtpGlobal`,
`emailOtpAttempt`, `emailChange`, `billingSync`, `ai`, `aiBudget`, `admin`, `invitation`,
`invitationGlobal` and `agentRegistrationGlobal` (see `DEFAULT_LIMITS` in
`nuxt-backend/rate-limit`). A limit of your own, enforced with `throws: true`, reads the same way.
