This page lists every default rate limit, and every window and lifetime the package enforces, so you can tell when a refusal or a deletion is expected. Every number in the generated tables is read from the code that enforces it, so the page and the deployment cannot disagree.

## What changes

- **New limits arrive with their units,** each listed here from `DEFAULT_LIMITS` once its unit lands: reports (SAFETY). The contact form and inbound mail have theirs: `contact`, `contactGlobal`, `inboundMail` and `inboundMailGlobal`.
- **The deletion grace period** is `setupAccount({ graceMs })`, 30 days by default, in the [Windows and lifetimes](#windows-and-lifetimes) below.
- **New windows arrive with their units:** the credit hold of a queued job, which lasts as long as the job waits, and the per-tier job budget, credits of jobs per owner per window aligned to UTC, named `jobBudget` (JOBS), and the retention you declare per table with `defineRetention` (RETAIN).

## Rate limits

`DEFAULT_LIMITS` from `nuxt-backend/rate-limit` guards the flows the package drives. Each limit is keyed where it is checked (per address, per billing entity, per caller), and the `*Global` ones are not keyed at all. Pass your own limits to `setupRateLimiter` to change one; a spent limit refuses with [`rate_limited`](/docs/reference/backend/errors#rate_limited), naming it.
