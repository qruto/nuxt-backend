This page lists every route the package mounts, who calls it, what guards it and the option that moves it, so you can tell a package route from one of yours and know which to open in a firewall or a proxy. The package mounts routes in two places. On the deployment, `registerBackendRoutes` in `backend/http.ts` mounts every inbound route at once: a part left out fails the push, not the first request. In the Nuxt app, the module adds the auth proxy, the health route and the ready-made pages.

## What changes

`0.2.0` adds the pages of the new features:

| Route | Where | Change | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/email/unsubscribe` | Deployment | Arrives: `POST` from a mail client's one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058), named by the `List-Unsubscribe` header of every non-transactional email | PREFS |
| `/email/events` | Deployment | Also takes inbound email: Resend's `email.received` and `inbox.email.received` events, which the package routes, checks and stores before your handlers see them | |
| `/invite` | App | New: checks an access link's token from the fragment and sends the person on to sign in (`pages.invite`) | |
| `/waitlist` | App | New: a person's place on the waitlist, from the signed token in the link's fragment (`pages.waitlist`) | |
| `/unsubscribe` | App | Arrives: the unsubscribe page a link in an email opens (`pages.unsubscribe`) | PREFS |
| `/gifts` | App | New: the gifts a person sent and received (`pages.gifts`) | |
| `/r/<code>` | App | New: a referral link. It says who invited the visitor and what they get, sends them to sign in and back for the claim step (`pages.referral`), and stores nothing on the device | |
| `/welcome` | App | New: the onboarding steps a new account sees once after its first sign-in (`pages.welcome`) | |
| `/admin` | App | Answers 404 to anyone without the role (`hidden`), shows your own tabs (pages below it), and gains the tabs of the new features | ACCESS, WAITLIST, JOBS, SAFETY |

Every private page of the package is kept out of search indexes with `noindex`. A guarded page is guarded by `definePageMeta({ auth })` alone.

Both tables below are generated from the code that mounts the routes, as it stands.

## On the deployment

The deployment's site origin (`*.convex.site`, or your custom domain) serves these:

<!-- generated:deployment-routes (pnpm docs:lookups, from src/convex/integrations/http.ts) -->

| Route | Methods | Called by | Guarded by | Moved with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `/api/auth/*` | GET, POST | The app's auth proxy | Better Auth: sessions, codes and the route limits `setupAuth` sets | `backend.authRoute` in nuxt.config, through the paths file |
| `/billing/events` | POST | The billing provider | A signed webhook: fail-closed without its secret (503), 1 MiB body cap (413), redelivery deduplicated | `billingPath` |
| `/email/events` | POST | The email provider | A signed webhook: fail-closed without its secret (503), 1 MiB body cap (413), redelivery deduplicated | `emailPath` |
| `/ai/stream` | POST, OPTIONS | The browser, through `useAiStream` | A session token; spends credits through the metered stream it starts | `aiPath` |
| `/storage/<id>` | GET | Whoever holds a URL `storage.signedUrl` minted | A signature under the app's secret that expires (an hour by default, seven days at most); mounted with `registerBackendRoutes(http, { storage })` | fixed |
| `/backend/health` | GET | The app's `/api/backend/health`, or an uptime check | Public: each capability's state only; the detail for a session of `adminRoles`, or on a deployment named `development` | fixed |

<!-- /generated:deployment-routes -->

Every webhook shares one fail-closed edge: an invalid signature answers `403`, an authentic event of a type the package does not know `202`, a handler that throws `500` (so the provider retries), and the outcome lands in the [delivery log](/docs/ship/security/webhooks).
