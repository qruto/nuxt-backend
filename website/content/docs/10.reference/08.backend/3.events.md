This page lists every event the backend records or receives, who reads each one and what the package does with it, so you know what your handlers get. Two kinds of events reach your backend. The **backend events** are the package's own: sign-ins, passkeys, workspace invitations and roles, an administrator's impersonation. Each is recorded on the audit trail where it happens, read back as activity, and handed to your `onEvent` action. The **provider events** arrive at the billing and email webhooks.

## What changes

- **New kinds:** a sign-in from a new device (`auth.new_device`), a lifted email suppression, a replayed webhook delivery, and user reports and their decisions.
- **Inbound email reaches your code.** `email.received` and `inbox.email.received` are routed, checked for spoofing and stored by the package first (reply addresses, workspace addresses and the support inbox), then reach your `events` handler. The `contact.*` events keep each person's topic preferences in step.
- **Suppressions are mirrored.** `suppression.added`, `suppression.removed` and `email.suppressed` keep the deployment's copy of the provider's suppression list, so sign-in tells a person their address cannot receive mail before it sends a code ([Handle suppressed addresses](/docs/build/email/delivery/suppressions)).
- **Both webhooks verify the same way.** One verifier, `standardwebhooks`, checks both providers' signatures, and the billing webhook calls the package's own handlers.

The tables below are generated from the event catalogs as they stand.

## Backend events

Each kind is recorded by the route or hook its row names, as one row of the audit trail that holds ids, never an address or a name. **Read by** says whose activity lists it, and **Derives** what the channel matrix lets it notify beside its row. The [notifications](/docs/build/email/notifications) capability delivers the in-app ones, and a billing, gift or referral kind never derives an email, because the billing provider emails its own customers. Kinds of your own live under `app.`, such as `app.project_archived`. [Record and review activity](/docs/build/auth/admin/audit-log) shows how to record one and read the trail.

## Provider events

Both webhooks accept the provider's whole event catalog, so a verified delivery is never lost: the package does its own work first, then calls the handler you passed as `events` to `setupBilling` or `setupEmail`, keyed by the provider's event name. An event newer than the package goes to `onUnknownEvent` and is answered `202`. [Hooks and guarantees](/docs/reference/backend/hooks#setupbilling-events) says how often a handler runs.
