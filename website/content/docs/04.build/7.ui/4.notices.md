Some messages hold for the whole session, on every page: an administrator is acting as the account, the app is under maintenance, a gift is waiting. `<BackendNotices>` is one region for all of them. Place it once in your layout; it renders nothing until a notice applies, then shows each one in order of importance. Each notice has a named slot, so you can reword or restyle one and keep the rest. The state behind each notice comes from the backend: the session, the maintenance flag, the billing provider's subscription status and the credit balance. The package adds the one region, the order, and the accessible announcement.

## Place it in your layout

```vue [app/layouts/default.vue]
<template>
  <div>
    <header><!-- your navigation --></header>
    <BackendNotices />
    <slot />
  </div>
</template>
```

The packaged pages render `<BackendNotices>` in their [frame](/docs/build/ui/pages#the-frame), and the [app shell](/docs/build/ui/app-shell) renders it under its header. A packaged page inside your layout leaves the notices to yours, so each one shows and is announced once.

## The notices

Most important first:

| Notice | Shows while | Slot | Arrives with |
|---|---|---|---|
| An administrator's visit | An administrator is [acting as the account](/docs/build/auth/admin/impersonation): whose account, when the visit ends, and a way out | `impersonation` | Built |
| Maintenance | The [maintenance switch](/docs/ship/operate/flags) is on, with the administrator's message | `maintenance` | Built |
| Payment past due | The subscription's last payment failed and the provider is retrying: the plan's features stay on during the grace period, with a button that opens the billing portal to update the card. Only to the billing managers | `past-due` | Built |
| Deletion scheduled | The account's [deletion](/docs/build/auth/accounts/data#delete-an-account) waits out its grace period: when it happens, and a **Keep my account** button | `deletion` | Built |
| Low credits | The balance is under the threshold, with a top-up | `low-credits` | Built |
| A gift to claim | A [gift](/docs/build/growth/offers/gifts) addressed to the person waits, with a claim button | `gift` | Built |
| Announcements | An [announcement](/docs/build/growth/engage/announcements) you published is live and not dismissed, with a **Dismiss** button | `announcement` | Built |

| Prop | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| `threshold` | `appConfig.backend.billing.lowCreditsThreshold`, `10` | The balance under which the low-credits notice shows |

## Replace one notice

A named slot replaces that notice and receives its state and actions. Leave the others to the component:

```vue [app/layouts/default.vue]
<template>
  <div>
    <BackendNotices>
      <template #low-credits="{ balance, topUp, dismiss }">
        <p>Only {{ balance }} credits left for new strips.</p>
        <button type="button" @click="topUp()">Add credits</button>
        <button type="button" @click="dismiss()">Not now</button>
      </template>
      <template #gift />
    </BackendNotices>
    <slot />
  </div>
</template>
```

An empty slot, like `#gift` above, turns that notice off. A slot of yours keeps what the notice promised: say it in words, keep a busy button focusable, and announce a failure as an alert ([Accessibility](/docs/build/ui/accessibility#what-a-replacement-must-keep)). Its words otherwise come from the `impersonation`, `maintenance`, `deletion`, `credits`, `gift` and `announcement` [label groups](/docs/build/ui/customize/labels#the-groups), and from `pastDue` once it lands.

## What it guarantees

One polite live region stays mounted, and each notice appears inside it, so a screen reader announces it. Each notice is a region named in words, never by colour alone. A dismissed notice leaves focus where it was. Nothing renders, and nothing is read, while no notice applies.

## What happens when…

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Two notices apply at once | Both show, most important first |
| A person dismisses low credits | It stays hidden until the balance rises above the threshold and falls again |
| The past-due payment succeeds on a retry | The notice disappears on every open page |
| The past-due grace period ends without payment | The subscription is canceled by the provider; the notice gives way to the free plan |

## Limits

- **Not for page-level messages.** A form's outcome belongs next to the form, not in the app-wide region.
- **Past-due needs a subscription.** It follows the billing provider's status; credit-only apps never show it ([React to billing events](/docs/build/billing/events)).

## Test it

Turn the maintenance switch on in the admin console's Flags tab: every page shows the maintenance notice, and a screen reader announces it once. In a component test, mount `<BackendNotices>` over a mock backend whose subscription status is `past_due` ([Test pages over a mock backend](/docs/develop/testing/pages)).
