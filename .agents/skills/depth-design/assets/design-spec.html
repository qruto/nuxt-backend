Principles

✓ do

  • keep elevation tiny and consistent
  • derive shadow + highlight from the surface
  • one orange glow per view
  • rebalance for dark mode, keep light direction

✕ don't

  • big soft drop-shadows on resting content
  • raw white or pure black on tints
  • fill large areas with orange
  • mix light directions
